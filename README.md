## Halibel — Your Personal AI Companion

    More than an assistant. A companion that understands your environment, helps you get things done, and stays by your side.

Halibel is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed to become part of your everyday digital life. Its purpose goes beyond answering questions: it aims to accompany you, understand your needs, interact with your digital environment, and help you manage everyday tasks more naturally and efficiently.

Whether you need help organizing your day, finding information, working with files, solving problems, or simply having someone to talk to, Halibel is designed to be there.

## ✨ What is Halibel?

Halibel is a personal AI assistant that combines conversational intelligence with access to your digital environment.

Unlike traditional chatbots, Halibel aims to provide a more contextual and interactive experience. It can assist with everyday activities, work with your files, understand instructions, and progressively become a more capable digital companion.

The vision is simple:

    Technology should not only respond to you. It should help you move forward.

Imagine an assistant that doesn't just wait for instructions.

## 🛠️ Development Philosophy

Halibel is built around a few fundamental principles:

 - User-centric: Designed around real user needs.
 - Context-aware: Understanding context is just as important as understanding instructions.
 - Autonomous, but controlled: Automation should always respect user permissions.
 - odular: Designed to evolve with new capabilities and integrations.
 - Transparent: Users should understand what the assistant can access and what actions it performs.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Access to personal files and the desktop environment requires responsible handling.

Halibel's development prioritizes:

 - Explicit permissions for sensitive operations.
 - Controlled access to local resources.
 - Clear distinction between reading and modifying files.
 - User authorization before executing potentially destructive actions.
 - Protection of personal information.
